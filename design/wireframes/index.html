01 · Callouts

AI = what the AI does · You = what you control · Why · undo = how its choice is explained and reversed.

  1. 1

    Ask Kairos… (Ctrl+K)

    AITurns a sentence into structured constraints, or into a question about your calendar.

    YouEdit the chips before anything runs. Nothing changes until you press Book or Apply.

    Why · undoThe chips show what was understood. Every booking ends in an Undo toast.

  2. 2

    + New meeting

    AIOpens the Schedule assistant, prefilled from your preferences, and ranks real free slots.

    YouAttendees, duration, date window, time of day, the focus rule.

    Why · undoEach slot lists ✓ reasons and ⚠ trade-offs. Undo after booking.

  3. 3

    Conflict badge and ⚠ outlines

    AIDetects overlaps, impossible travel and focus intrusions, and ranks them by severity.

    YouOpen the Conflict center, apply a fix, or keep both on purpose.

    Why · undoType, severity and events are named, and every fix is previewed before Apply. Shown red in the app; the shape and ⚠ carry the meaning here.

  4. 4

    Meeting-load bar per day

    AITotals meeting hours per day against your limit (5 h) and feeds the overload forecast.

    YouChange the daily limit in Settings.

    Why · undoHours are printed next to the bar. Display only; nothing is changed.

  5. 5

    Protected focus (hatched, 🔒)

    AIThe focus guardian keeps focus blocks free; at autonomy "Auto" it adds blocks toward your weekly goal (✨).

    YouFocus goal and autonomy level; drag, unlock or delete any block.

    Why · undo"Protected by Kairos" opens the reason. Undo from the toast or the audit log.

  6. 6

    ✨ AI-created event

    AIMarks every event it created or booked for you.

    YouEdit it like any other event; the sparkle stays as provenance.

    Why · undoDetails show the original request and the slot's reasons. Undo through the audit log.

  7. 7

    Ghost preview

    AIShows a suggestion as a dashed block before it exists (here: prep for Friday's QBR).

    YouAccept, move or ignore it. Ghosts never notify anyone.

    Why · undoHover shows the reason. Nothing to undo until you accept.

  8. 8

    Copilot · Needs attention

    AIOne card per conflict, with the best-ranked fix and its impact.

    YouPreview, Apply, or open all fixes in the Conflict center.

    Why · undo"Affects 4 people" and the reasons are on the card. Undo toast after Apply.

  9. 9

    Copilot · Insights

    AIForecasts risk (focus at risk next week) and flags recurring meetings that routinely end early.

    YouAct (Protect blocks, Draft message) or dismiss.

    Why · undo"Why?" opens the evidence: forecast band, 38-minute average, cost. Applied changes are undoable.

  10. 10

    Copilot · Up next

    AIShows the next meetings with a prep summary and a smart-reminder plan sized to each one.

    YouOpen the brief, snooze, accept or edit the plan.

    Why · undoEvery reminder carries its "because…". Reset to the default at any time.

  11. 11

    👍 / 👎 on every AI card

    AILearns from thumbs and from which slot rank you pick (preference learning).

    YouRate any card. 👎 asks for a one-tap reason.

    Why · undoFeedback is logged with the card's inputs; learned preferences can be reset in Settings.

  12. 12

    AI status pill

    AINames the engine in use: "Claude · claude-opus-5-5" or "Offline AI" (heuristic parser and templates).

    YouClick it for Settings › AI status: test the connection, read the call log.

    Why · undoIf Claude fails, Kairos falls back to offline mode and says so; every feature keeps working.

02 · Callouts

The LLM interprets; deterministic engines find slots and compute numbers.

  1. 1

    Natural-language input

    AIWorks out the intent (schedule, find conflicts, ask about time) and the details, with one schema-checked Claude call or the offline parser.

    YouType freely; ↑ recalls history; Esc closes with no side effects.

    Why · undoTyping alone never changes the calendar.

  2. 2

    Interpretation chips

    AIShows what it understood: attendees, duration, date window, time of day.

    YouClick or Tab to a chip to change or remove it; results re-rank instantly.

    Why · undoThe chips are the explanation. A wrong chip is fixed here, before anything is booked.

  3. 3

    Engine badge

    AINames the engine that read the request: Claude with its latency, or Offline.

    YouRetry with Claude when offline; details in Settings › AI status.

    Why · undoEvery call is logged with latency and outcome.

  4. 4

    Ranked slots, inline

    AIThe scheduling engine (not the LLM) finds and scores real free slots; Claude only words the reasons.

    You↑↓ to choose (ghost on the calendar), Enter to book, Ctrl+Enter for the full assistant.

    Why · undoScore plus ✓ / ⚠ on every row. Booking has a confirm step and ends in an Undo toast.

  5. 5

    Conflict and analytics answers

    AIThe same bar answers questions. Numbers come from the analytics engine, never from the LLM.

    YouJump to the Conflict center or Insights from the answer.

    Why · undoEach answer says where its numbers come from. Read-only.

  6. 6

    Offline fallback and clarifying

    AIIf Claude is unavailable, the heuristic parser takes over. Anything it is unsure of becomes a dashed chip that asks.

    YouPick the right person or value, or retry with Claude.

    Why · undoThe Offline badge stays visible; nothing is guessed silently.

  7. 7

    Feedback

    AILogs 👍 / 👎 with the request and its interpretation; a 👎 becomes a new eval case.

    YouOne tap, optional reason.

    Why · undoNever changes the calendar.

03 · Callouts

The solver picks the times; the AI explains them. Nothing is booked without your confirm.

  1. 1

    Prefilled constraints

    AIFills the form from the command (or from your preferences); AI-filled fields are tinted.

    YouChange any field; the list re-ranks live.

    Why · undoTinted vs plain fields show what came from the AI and what came from you.

  2. 2

    Respect focus time

    AITreats focus blocks as hard constraints while the toggle is on.

    YouSwitch it off for this one meeting.

    Why · undoWith it off, slots that use focus time are labeled as such.

  3. 3

    Rank and score bar

    AIThe solver scores each candidate on preferences, fairness, buffers and focus; rank 1 is best.

    YouPick any slot, not just #1 (the rank you pick is tracked).

    Why · undo"Score breakdown" lists each factor's contribution.

  4. 4

    ✓ reasons and ⚠ warnings

    AIClaude (or a template offline) puts the solver's factors into plain words, including who pays the cost.

    YouWeigh the trade-offs before booking.

    Why · undoGreen ✓ and amber ⚠ in the app; the glyph always carries the meaning.

  5. 5

    Attendee local-time strip

    AIConverts the slot to each attendee's time zone and rates comfort against their working hours.

    YouSee who gets the late call (☾) before you book; fairness history is shown.

    Why · undoComfort colors in the app are backed by ✓ / ◐ / ☾ glyphs.

  6. 6

    Hover = ghost preview

    AIHovering or ↑↓-focusing a card draws the slot on the calendar.

    YouSee what comes before and after it without committing.

    Why · undoDashed and labeled "preview"; it disappears when you move away.

  7. 7

    No slot → relaxations

    AIWhen nothing fits, proposes the smallest changes and how many slots each one unlocks.

    YouChoose one, or none. It never relaxes a constraint silently.

    Why · undoEach option names the constraint it loosens.

  8. 8

    Confirm with an AI-drafted agenda

    AIDrafts a title and agenda from the request and recent shared meetings (titles only, if privacy allows).

    YouEdit or clear them; Confirm sends the invites.

    Why · undoLabeled "drafted by Claude" or "template".

  9. 9

    Undo toast

    AINothing new here; the toast is the safety net for what the AI proposed.

    YouUndo from the toast, or later from the audit log.

    Why · undoUndo cancels the event and notifies attendees; both actions are logged.

  10. 10

    Feedback

    AI👍 / 👎 and the rank you pick feed the preference learner.

    YouRate a slot; 👎 asks why (wrong time zone, too early, wrong day).

    Why · undoLearned weights can be reset in Settings.

04 · Callouts

Fixes are generated and ranked by the conflict engine; Claude only drafts the words.

  1. 1

    Conflict list

    AIDetects overlaps, impossible travel and focus intrusions (plus overload and outside-hours warnings), with severity from each event's priority.

    YouFilter by severity, expand one, or keep both on purpose.

    Why · undoEach item names its type (icon + word), severity (a word, not just a color), date and events.

  2. 2

    Ranked fixes

    AIBuilds candidate fixes (move, shorten, make virtual, decline) and ranks them by priority and disruption.

    YouChoose any option, not just #1.

    Why · undoScore bar and rank; the factors behind each score are listed.

  3. 3

    Impact and reasons

    AICounts who is affected and lists ✓ benefits and ⚠ costs for each fix.

    YouCompare "affects 4 people" with "affects 1 person" before choosing.

    Why · undoImpact is computed from the attendee list, not estimated by the LLM.

  4. 4

    Drafted note

    AIClaude drafts a short, polite note from the event facts (external names redacted if that setting is on).

    YouEdit it before sending; nothing is sent without your click.

    Why · undoLabeled "drafted by Claude" or "template"; sent notes are listed in the audit log.

  5. 5

    Preview on the calendar

    AIShows the fix in place: the new time as a ghost, plus a banner with its impact.

    YouApply or cancel from the banner or the card; jump back to the original day.

    Why · undoA preview is local: nobody is notified until Apply.

  6. 6

    Apply → Undo

    AIApplies the change and notifies the attendees.

    YouUndo from the toast, or later from the audit log.

    Why · undoUndo restores the original time and sends a correction.

  7. 7

    Keep both, with feedback

    AIStops flagging that pair and learns from the reason you give.

    YouKeep both, optionally with a one-tap reason.

    Why · undoThe dismissal is logged and can be reverted.

  8. 8

    Autonomy for conflicts

    AIAt "Auto", applies fixes that change only your own calendar and announces them.

    YouChoose Suggest, Ask or Auto in Settings › Autonomy.

    Why · undoEvery auto-fix comes with Why? and Undo, and is logged.
